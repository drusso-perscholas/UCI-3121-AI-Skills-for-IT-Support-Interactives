What a Strong Decision Looks Like

Publish or hold, decide and justify

Strong decision, names the call, then grounds it in evidence

"Publish - V1 to V5 all passed, and the reader-path test showed zero stalls across all five steps, so a first-time reader could follow it without guessing. Nothing to fix before it goes live."

"Hold - V1 to V5 all passed, but the reader-path test showed two stalls on unlabelled buttons in steps 3 and 5, so a first-time reader would likely fail. Fix the labels, then publish."

Decision, stated first Cites the V1 to V5 gate Cites the hesitation log

Weak decision, asserts the call, cites nothing

"Publish. It is ready to go."

No reference to the log or the gate
